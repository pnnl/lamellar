# Triangle Count implementation notes

The maintained `triangle_count` executable loads the distributed graph through
`graph/mod.rs` and its map-backed representation in `graph/mapgraph.rs`.

Use `--variants single buffered am-group` to compare the active-message
strategies. `--buffer-size` applies to buffered and AM-group variants, and
`--launch-threads` partitions local work. Results and build/runtime provenance
are emitted by PE 0 for every selected configuration and iteration.

The previous standalone single and buffered implementations are archived under
`src/deprecated/`; they are not Cargo targets.
